Adina's Plov Recipe

Plov

Recipe by: Adina Nurlanova

Ingredients list

  • 1 kg of long-grain rice
  • 1 kg of lamb
  • 1 kg of carrots
  • 300 ml vegetable oil
  • 4 small onion
  • 2 heads of garlic
  • 3 tbsp of salt

Directions

  1. Wash the rice 2-3 times until the water is clean.
  2. Cut the lamb into small cubes.
  3. Cut the onions into half-rings.
  4. Cut the carrots into long strips 1cm thick.
  5. Peel the garlic, removing the outer husk, but do not seperate into cloves.
  6. Heat the Dutch oven, pour in the oil, and let it heat.Add the onion and fry it well until it turns a dark golden color.
  7. Add the lamb to the onions, and cook for about 10-15 minutes.
  8. Add carrots to the Dutch oven.
  9. Add salt.
  10. Bring the heat to medium and let it cook until carrots are soft.
  11. Pour the water just until it covers the ingridients, and let it boil.
  12. Reduce the heat and simmer for 1 hour.
  13. Spread the rice evenly, increase the heat to maximum, add more water so that it covers the rice with a 3-cm layer.
  14. As soon as all the water is absorbed, press the head of garlics into the rice and cover the lid.
  15. Reduce the heat to minimum and leave the plov on stove for 30 minutes.
  16. Now is ready!
